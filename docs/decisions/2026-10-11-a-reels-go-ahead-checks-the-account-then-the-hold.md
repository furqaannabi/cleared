# A Reel's go-ahead checks the account can be read, then the hold

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
On YouTube the creator gives their unlisted video with the request for the go-ahead, so a wrong file is found before anything is public ([record](2026-10-10-the-unlisted-video-is-given-with-the-go-ahead.md)). Instagram has no unlisted upload: there is nothing to look at until the Reel is live.

## Options
1. **The account, then the hold.** Cleared checks it can still read the creator's Instagram account, then the money path re-confirms the hold. The file is first matched at the live check.
2. **The hold only.** No Instagram read at the go-ahead. Simpler, but a creator whose connection has expired is told to post and learns only afterwards that Cleared cannot read their account.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- A Reel's go-ahead takes no link.
- A creator whose access is lost is told to reconnect before they post, and PayPal is asked nothing.
- A creator who posts the wrong file finds out after it is public. That cannot be fixed on Instagram, so the brand decides whether to accept it. This is a risk YouTube deals do not carry, and it is accepted.
