# Who can watch a draft, and how long it is kept

**Date:** 2026-10-09
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan (William to agree)
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
Drafts are unpublished videos, the most sensitive thing Cleared holds. CLAUDE.md requires them private, reached only through short-lived addresses, and seen by a brand only in its own deal. How long they are kept, and when the brand may see one, was not set.

## Options
1. **The latest draft only, deleted 30 days after the deal ends.** The creator can always watch their latest draft. The brand can watch it only once it has something to do on it. An older file is deleted when a newer draft finishes checking.
2. **Keep every draft until the deal ends.** Useful if a dispute ever needed an older draft, but nothing in the product shows earlier drafts.
3. **Delete as soon as the draft is approved.** Least held, but the publish step needs the approved file, and the brand could not re-watch what it approved.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- An address that plays a draft works for 15 minutes and can be refreshed.
- The brand is shown a draft only from the first ask or the start of the review window of that run, and only inside its own deal.
- When a newer draft's run finishes, the older file and what was read from it are deleted.
- Everything kept for a post's drafts is deleted 30 days after the post is paid, released or closed. A demo account's drafts go when the account does.
- The bucket also deletes anything older than 90 days as a backstop, set by Furqaan when the bucket is made.
- Speech and on-screen text read from a draft are treated like the draft: never logged, and deleted with it.
