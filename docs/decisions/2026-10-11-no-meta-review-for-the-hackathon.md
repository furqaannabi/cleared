# No Meta review of the app for the hackathon

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan (William to agree)
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
Until Meta has reviewed an app, Instagram lets only accounts the team adds as testers connect to it. A review needs a verified business and can take weeks. Submission is 12 November.

## Options
1. **No review for the hackathon.** The app stays in development mode. Instagram works for the team's testers and, through the shared account, for every demo visitor.
2. **Ask for the review now.** If it lands in time real creators can connect; it costs time on forms and a screen recording, and Meta may ask for changes.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- Real creators cannot connect Instagram yet. The spec says so as a limit, and so does PRODUCT.md.
- Nothing in the build waits on Meta's timing.
- A review can be asked for later without changing the code.
