# Lost YouTube access decides nothing

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
The go-ahead and the live check read YouTube with the creator's stored read-only access. That access can stop working: the creator revokes it, or it expires. While the Google app is in Testing every stored access dies after 7 days, and a deadline can be 21 days away. What a deal does when Cleared cannot read the channel was not set.

## Options
1. **The creator reconnects; nothing is decided meanwhile.** The go-ahead is refused and no live check runs, each saying "reconnect YouTube". No answer goes to the money path.
2. **Treat it as "cannot decide".** Keeps deals moving, but a creator could revoke access on purpose to skip the live check and be paid on the brand's silence.
3. **Treat it as a fixable failure.** Starts a clock the moment access is lost, even if the creator reconnects within the hour.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- A check that could not read YouTube gives the money path no answer, for any reason.
- The creator's post says to reconnect YouTube. The check runs once they have.
- The money path's deadline runs meanwhile. A post nobody could check by then is released like any other.
- The Google app must leave Testing before real deals run. This was already on PRODUCT.md's list of risks.
