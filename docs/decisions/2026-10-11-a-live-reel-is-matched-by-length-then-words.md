# A live Reel is matched to the approved draft by its length, then its words

**Date:** 2026-10-11
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
PRODUCT.md ties a live Reel to its draft by transcript and length, without saying what counts as a match. Instagram re-encodes every upload, so file size cannot be used as it is on YouTube. A Reel with no speech has no transcript to compare.

## Options
1. **Length, then words; the unclear goes to the brand.** Cleared fetches the Reel and reads its speech once. Within 2 seconds of the draft's length and at least 90% of the draft's words in the same order is a match. More than 2 seconds off, or under half the words, is not the approved file. Anything between, or a draft with too little speech, cannot be decided.
2. **Length only.** No second reading: cheaper and faster. But any other video of the same length would pass.
3. **Check the live Reel against the whole checklist again.** The strongest proof, but it doubles the AI cost of every Reel and can fail a post on an item the brand already approved.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- The match is fixed code with stated limits. No model decides it.
- "Not the approved file" cannot be fixed, so the brand has 48 hours to accept it. "Cannot decide" goes to the brand to confirm, and silence pays, by [the rule already recorded](2026-10-10-which-live-check-finding-gives-which-answer.md).
- The paid-partnership label and a Reel whose video cannot be fetched are also "cannot decide", by that same record: they are things the platform would not show.
- Each Reel is read by Data Automation once more. The match is decided once and kept, so checking a fixed caption again reads nothing twice.
- The limits (2 seconds, 90%, 50%, 20 words) are settings, to be tried against real Reels.
