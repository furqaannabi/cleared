# Deleting an account removes what the creator made and keeps anonymous money records

**Date:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan (William to agree)
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
William's Settings page offers "Delete account" and promises that the profile, connected accounts and drafts are deleted while the payment records Cleared must keep stay, with the name and email removed. It did not say what happens to the rest of a finished deal: the brief, the checklist, notes, check results and the brand's email. Nor was it set what a creator who cannot sign in does, or how fast.

## Options
1. **Delete the content, keep the money skeleton.** Everything the creator made or said goes. One anonymous row per post stays: amounts, dates, stage, platform, the brand's name and PayPal references. The brand's links to those deals stop working.
2. **Keep the whole deal and anonymise the creator.** The brand's link still shows the finished deal, but more of what the creator wrote and said is kept than the page promises.
3. **Delete everything but PayPal references.** Least kept, but a record could no longer be tied to a deal if PayPal or the brand asked about it.

## Decision
Option 1. A request by email is done within 30 days by Furqaan, with a command that runs the same code as the route.

## Consequences
- Deleting is refused while a post is held, a payout is not settled, or a brand may still hold. The answer lists what is in the way, deal by deal. Nothing is left for the money path to finish for a creator who is gone.
- Deleted: the profile, connected accounts and tokens, sessions, drafts and everything read from them, each deal's brief, checklist, questions, notes, terms, check and live-check results, the brand's email, and every link.
- Kept: each held post's money records, with the deal's and post's ids, the brand's name and the platform. The creator's name, email and PayPal email are removed from them. Amounts, dates, stages and references are never altered.
- The money path gains one function that removes the name and emails from a finished deliverable's record. No money rule changes.
- A deal can outlive its creator, so it no longer has to point at one.
- Records go at once, in one change. Files go by a job within 24 hours.
- Signing in again with the same Google account makes a new, empty account.
- William's confirmation wording should name the brief, checklist and check results among what is deleted.
- A brand asking for its own email to be deleted while the creator's account exists is done by hand. Nothing is built for it.
