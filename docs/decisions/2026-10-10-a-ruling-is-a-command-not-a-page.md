# A ruling is a command, not a page

**Date:** 2026-10-10
**Status:** Accepted
**Decided by:** Furqaan
**Supersedes:** none

## Context
When a brand objects to a live post that Cleared could not decide on, the rules say a person at Cleared rules: pay or release. The money path has the function and releases on day 28 if nobody rules. Nobody at Cleared has any way to see what is waiting or to rule.

## Options
1. **A command.** Two commands run by a person with access to the service: one lists what waits, one records the ruling and who made it. No page and no admin sign-in to secure.
2. **An admin page behind a secret.** Easier to use, but a new public surface that can pay or release money, needing its own sign-in and its own spec.
3. **Nobody rules; day 28 releases.** Nothing to build, but a brand could always get its money back by objecting.

## Decision
Option 1.

## Consequences
- Rulings are made by Furqaan or William from a terminal, with the name of who ruled recorded in the money record.
- There is no route that rules. Nothing a visitor can reach pays or releases on a person's say.
- The list shows the brand's objection and the live check's findings. Both are untrusted text and are shown as text.
- A post nobody rules on is released on day 28, as the money path already does.
- An admin page can replace the commands later without changing the money path.
