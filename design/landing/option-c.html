How a deal runs

01Agree the checklist

You and the brand agree the checklist the AI drew from the brief. Every item cites its line.

02Money held in PayPal

The brand’s money is reserved, not taken. You can see it before you film.

03AI checks your draft

Item by item, with timestamps you can play. Unsure goes to a person, never a guess.

0448-hour review

The brand reviews before you publish. Silence clears a fully passing draft.

05Publish, get paid

The live check confirms the post, and the hold is paid out to you.

Rules that protect you

The AI never moves money

It finds evidence. Fixed code decides and calls PayPal.

Unsure goes to a person

Never rounded up to a pass, never a guess.

A missed deadline returns the hold

Fair both ways: no post, no payment.

Only a full pass clears on a timer

Anything failed or unsure waits for you or the brand.

For the brand

Your money is held, not paid, until the approved post is live and checked. You review the draft before it’s published.
