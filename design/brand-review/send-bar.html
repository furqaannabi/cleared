One send bar for the brand’s notes

Once the brand saves a first note, a bar is pinned to the bottom of the screen with the count and Send. Tapping Send opens the confirmation in the bar itself, listing each item and its note. On phones it takes the place of the Agree panel or the review dock until the notes are sent or removed. Agree and Approve move into the page, under the items. The right column no longer lists unsent notes. The same bar is used for objections in the draft review (“Add objection”) and for changes on the checklist (“Ask for a change”). Each look is shown with two objections saved, then with the confirmation open, at 390 px and on desktop, and finally on the checklist step. Example data.
