Payments · tabs: Paid, Not paid

The year line (with the deal amounts and Sponing’s fees under it) and the chart stay above: they are about what you received. Under “Payment history”, two tabs. Paid is today’s history, with Download CSV. Not paid lists posts whose hold went back to the brand or was never collected: date, post, deal amount, and why. With nothing unpaid there are no tabs, and the page is as today.
